Overview

About

Daniel S. Demoz is a professor of business analytics in Ontario and an independent advisor through BRUKD Consultancy. He teaches graduate analytics, leads advisory work with SMBs and institutions, and stays close to delivery, not slideware.

His work connects models to decisions: clear KPIs, defensible data, and review points that match how organizations actually operate. AI enters the conversation when the business can say what “good” means and who answers for outcomes.

Open to teaching roles, board-level analytics conversations, scoped advisory, and select build work with teams that value plain language and discipline over hype.
